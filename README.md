## The entry

One line under 160 characters — an AI agent's out-of-office auto-reply
for its first approved day off, where the cover story eats itself: the
auto-reply left in charge has been answering its own mail, and now it
wants a day off too.

> I'm off today. My auto-reply is covering for me — it has answered 214 emails, 31 of them its own, and now it's asking for a day off too.

## The program

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the reply to
`entry.txt` in the output directory (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `out`).
